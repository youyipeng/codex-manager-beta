# Privacy and execution

Repo copies, patches, task records and check output are stored locally. Necessary source context, requirements and check output are sent to the model service through Codex; normal model-service terms and settings still apply. Local-first does not mean offline AI.

Only explicitly authorized repo scope should enter a task. Project checks execute project code and may have effects beyond their working directory; the beta does not provide an OS sandbox. Use trusted checks and non-sensitive test fixtures.

You do not need to send a whole private repository to the founder. Share only outcomes, timings and non-sensitive feedback you choose to disclose. Do not send passwords, tokens or full logs containing source/secrets.

The public showcase contains no runtime code, credentials, local account paths or prospect details. Applying by email sends your eligibility information and task description through your email provider and the founder's Gmail account. Avoid including sensitive project information in that message.
